Sahayak v137 - Reply streaming (delta on top of v136)

WHAT CHANGED
- The tutor's reply now appears word by word on the lesson screen instead of all at once after a wait. Families said the app felt slow; this is the biggest "feels fast" fix.
- Works with Groq, Gemini and any OpenAI-style provider in your AI registry. Marking answers, reading photos and every JSON call are NOT streamed.
- Nothing is saved until the whole reply has arrived, exactly as before.
- If a provider fails part-way, the half-written reply disappears and the next provider writes it from the start.
- If streaming is unavailable for any reason, the screen falls back to the normal reply.
- No database change.

ORDER
1. Copy the files over your project and deploy the website. No migration.
2. Open a lesson, send a message and an answer. Check: words appear as they are written; the finished reply matches what you see after reloading the lesson; the next question appears after the reply.
3. Check /admin/ai-requests: the tutor calls should show provider and latency as before.
