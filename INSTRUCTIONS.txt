Sahayak v139 - Activity analytics (delta on top of v138)

WHAT CHANGED
- New page /admin/activity, in the admin menu as "Activity analytics" (Super Admin only). Choose 7, 30 or 90 days.
- Sign-ins: count, different people, failed attempts, wrong-portal, sign-outs, with a daily bar strip.
- Coming back: of the people who signed in before this window, how many signed in again.
- New accounts by role, with a daily strip.
- Lessons: started, completed, abandoned, finish rate (lessons still open are not counted as failures), different students.
- AI reliability per provider: calls, failures, fail %, typical speed and slow-1-in-20 speed (from calls that worked), and why calls failed in plain groups (too slow, overloaded, rate limit, key problem...). Raw error text is never shown.
- Counts only: no names, messages or answers. A panel without enough data says why instead of showing zero.
- No database change.
