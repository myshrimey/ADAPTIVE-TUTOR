SAHAYAK - v63 UPDATE  (Challenge zone)
Apply v62 (and everything before it) first.

WHAT THIS UPDATE DOES
1. CHALLENGE ZONE for strong students (new page: /stretch, card "Challenge zone" on the student dashboard, and a button on the adaptive-check result).
   - Harder questions written in the STYLE of olympiad, NTSE, Maths Olympiad (IOQM), JEE-foundation, NEET-foundation exams, and a general "challenge" style. They stay within Class 10 ideas; they are hard because of the thinking needed, not because they go into Class 11.
   - A topic opens only when the student has shown strong command of it (mastery score 75 or more in lessons/quizzes, or a demonstrated skill in the adaptive check). A student with nothing open sees a friendly "locked for now" message.
   - Three levels: Bridge, Challenge, Summit. A student moves up after 3 correct in a row and steps back after 2 wrong in a row. Each question is asked once per student. There is a daily limit (default 30).
   - A wrong answer is shown as normal ("these are meant to be very hard"). The right answer and solution appear only AFTER answering.
   - Answers here never change marks, mastery, readiness or the learning path.
2. BADGES: 9 badges (first solved, 10 solved, 50 solved, 3 in a row, 5 in a row, first top-level, 5 top-level, two exam styles, two subjects). They give NO points, money or discounts, as you ruled for referrals and rewards.
3. ADMIN > CHALLENGE ZONE: switch on/off; the mastery score needed to open a topic; how many correct/wrong move the level; the daily limit; whether students may see questions a teacher has not yet checked (they are told when a question is unchecked). A "Write questions" button asks the free AI to write Challenge questions for a subject (with the second independent check). They all stay "needs review".
4. TEACHERS, TUTORS, CENTRES can upload Challenge questions in the same upload form (tick the Challenge box, choose exam style and level). They wait for your review at Admin > Question register, with who uploaded and who reviewed, as before.
5. PARENTS, TUTORS, CENTRES see only counts (tried, solved, badges) on the baseline page - nothing about right/wrong per question.
6. Challenge questions are kept OUT of normal lessons, quizzes, mocks and the adaptive check.

STEPS
1. Unzip over your project folder (replace files).
2. npm install
3. npm run db:migrate   (adds migration 0040; safe to run twice)
4. Deploy as usual.
5. Open Admin > Challenge zone. Choose each subject and press "Write questions" (try 5 first). Then open Admin > Question register and approve or reject the new Challenge questions, or ask a teacher to.
6. Try /stretch with a student account that has a topic at 75%+ mastery.
