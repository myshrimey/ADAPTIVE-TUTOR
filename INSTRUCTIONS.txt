Apply after v122. Copy these files over your project (same folders), then deploy the website the way you normally do.

WHAT CHANGED (plain English)
- A new subject, French, on the home list (Pilot tag). Class X only.
- 19 chapters and 71 skills: nine grammar chapters (verbs, pronouns, negation, questions, indirect speech, relative pronouns, demonstratives, possessives, subjunctive), reading comprehension, writing tasks, and eight lessons from the Entre Jeunes textbook (lessons 2-8 and 10).
- 356 original multiple-choice questions (one short answer), all marked needs_review. Nothing is copied from the textbook. Verb answers were computed with a conjugation program, the rest were written by hand and spell-checked.
- When a student types a French answer with accents, a French letters keypad appears (e, accents, c-cedilla, oe...). Answers are checked strictly for accents, but a curly apostrophe and the two ways of storing an accent are both accepted.
- French is not translated to Hindi-medium; it has a planning blueprint (objective practice only).
