SAHAYAK - v62 UPDATE
Apply v61 (and everything before it) first.

WHAT THIS UPDATE DOES
1. ADAPTIVE CHECK (new page: /baseline, button "Find my starting point" on the student dashboard).
   - It does NOT have a fixed number of questions. It asks while it still needs evidence and stops when enough topics are settled (limits you can change: Admin > Adaptive diagnostic engine; defaults 15 minimum, about 20-30 usual, 50 maximum).
   - It starts at Class 10. A wrong answer is not a verdict: it first looks at why (a slip in working is re-checked with different numbers), then checks the prerequisite skills below it, class by class, only as far as needed.
   - Result: a skill picture by area (not one class label), the gaps and how serious they are, and a personal learning path that repairs only the missing prerequisites, then returns to Class 10. Every step says "why you are learning this".
   - Progress is saved after every answer; the student can stop and continue later.
   - Later quizzes add evidence to the same picture. A short re-check button re-tests only repaired and uncertain skills.
2. REPORTS: parent, tutor and centre pages show the starting point, gaps and path in careful wording (no "weak", no "Class 7 student"). Tutors and centres also get "common prerequisite gaps across your students" (shown only for 3 or more students, no names).
3. QUESTION REGISTER (Admin > Question register): every question with where it came from, who uploaded it, who reviewed it, teacher feedback, and which request it answers. You can approve or reject, upload a question yourself, and handle requests.
4. TEACHERS, TUTORS, CENTRES can add questions and ask for questions (link "Add questions or ask for questions" on their pages). Their questions always wait for your review; nobody except you can mark a question verified.
5. YOUR NEW RULE: every session in every subject (diagnostic, quiz, mock, learning session, adaptive check) now owes that subject at least 10 new questions, written in the background and kept as "needs review" with the source recorded. WHAT WAS TESTED (test setup, not your live site)
- Six simulated students (strong Class 10; mixed Class 9/10; strong basics but weak application; Class 8 prerequisite gaps; language difficulty; high marks but inconsistent reasoning): they got clearly different results. The strong one finished in 15 questions with no repeat of lower classes; the Class 8 one was routed to earlier-class prerequisites; a slip did not push anyone down a class; language difficulty created no serious gap; the check went past 20 when needed and stopped early when sure; every question choice has a stored reason.
- The database layer end to end (127 checks in total with provenance): resume, no answer key reaches the browser, no repeated question, saved baseline versions, learning path, practice, quiz evidence, short re-check.
- Provenance: uploads tagged by role, duplicates refused, a teacher cannot self-verify, reviews recorded, requests tracked, minimum 10 per session.
- Earlier suites still pass. Migration 0039 ran twice on a real Postgres. Type check and full build pass.
