SAHAYAK - v67 UPDATE  (tidy admin menu + Feedback Intelligence + status page)
Apply v66 (and everything before it) first.

WHAT THIS UPDATE DOES
1. A NEW ADMIN MENU. At the top of every admin page there is now one bar with 8 headings: Overview, Students & Learning, Content & Questions, AI, Money, Customers & Growth, Feedback, Settings & Safety. Click a heading to see its pages with a short line about each. A "Find a page" box finds any page by name (for example type "traffic" or "olympiad"). The page you are on is highlighted and shown as "Heading > Page". On a phone it folds into a "Menu" button. The Command Centre also has "Everything in one place", a fold-out list of all pages by heading.
2. FEEDBACK INTELLIGENCE (Admin > Feedback > Feedback Intelligence). Messages from students and parents are sorted by fixed rules into groups (for example "Translation problem - quiz"), with how many people reported it, how serious it looks, how much care a fix needs, a likely cause and a suggested action. These are GUESSES from the words people wrote and are labelled so. No AI is used, so it costs nothing.
   You decide with five buttons: Approve, Reject, Modify, Investigate, Defer. Later you can mark a group Implemented, Verified or Rolled back. For high-risk groups (scores, answers, plans, prices) Approve needs a written note and a tick "I have tested this". Pressing a button only records YOUR decision and keeps a history; it never changes a question, score, price or lesson.
   Admin > Feedback > All feedback messages is the plain list, now with the menu.
3. "WHAT IS BUILT / NOT BUILT" (Admin > Settings & Safety). A plain table of every part of your big "Learning Intelligence Platform" prompt marked BUILT, PARTIAL, NOT IMPLEMENTED or REQUIRES PROVIDER.
4. Migration 0043 (new feedback columns and two server-only tables). Safe to run twice.
