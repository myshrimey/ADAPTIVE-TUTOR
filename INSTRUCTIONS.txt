Sahayak v140 - View Account As Admin, read only (delta on top of v139)

WHAT CHANGED
- On /admin/accounts, open a STUDENT account: a new box "View as this student". Type a reason (8 to 200 characters) and press Start view.
- You get a read-only view for 15 minutes with a red banner ("Viewing account xxxx as admin. Read only.") and an Exit link. You stay signed in as yourself. It is never a login as the child and nothing can be changed from it.
- Three pages: Dashboard (grade, plan, subjects, latest lessons), Progress by subject, Report and tests. No name, contact details, messages or answers are shown.
- Every start (with your reason), every page opened and the exit are written to the Audit log (/admin/audit-log). At most 20 starts an hour. Super Admin only. Students only (parents, tutors and centres are not viewable).
- The ticket is signed with a key derived from your existing server key (no new setting), is tied to your sign-in and to that one account, and cannot be extended.
- One small change from the saved design: one ticket covers the three read-only pages of that one account (otherwise every page switch would need a new reason).
- The lawyer review is deferred (decision FD-16). Whether families must be told that staff can view is still open (D7).
- No database change.

ORDER
1. Copy the files over your project and deploy the website. No migration.
2. Open a test student's account, start a view with a reason, click through the three pages, press Exit.
3. Open /admin/audit-log and look for account.view_as_start (with your reason), account.view_as_page and account.view_as_end.
4. Wait 15 minutes (or use an old link): the page should say the ticket expired.
