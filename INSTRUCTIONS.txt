SAHAYAK v76 - Acquisition & Referral Intelligence Engine

1. Unzip over your project (same folders), commit, let Vercel deploy.
2. Run the database update:  npm run db:migrate   (adds migration 0048; safe to run again).
3. Open /admin/acquisition (also in the menu under Customers & Growth, and as a card on the Command Centre).
4. Social Media Growth Engine > Settings: make sure the website address is entered. Until then no post has a tracked link.
5. Try it: open your site with ?utm_source=facebook&utm_campaign=test&utm_content=my-post at the end of the address in a private window, sign up a test family, then look at /admin/acquisition. The test account should appear under Facebook, campaign test.
6. Partners: "Add or change records" > Add a partner. Share the link ending ?p=TUTOR-NAME-001. Rewards/commission stay your own decision.
7. Offline sales: record them with "Record an offline sale". It does NOT switch on access; do that at /admin/payments as before.
8. For cost per customer: record marketing spend and tag it to a source or campaign. Without that, it shows "Not available" (your own time is never counted as money).
