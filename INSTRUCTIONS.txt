SAHAYAK - v71 UPDATE  (what kind of mistakes, and a ready plan for each)
Apply v70 (and everything before it) first. NO database change.

WHAT THIS UPDATE DOES
A mistake is more than "wrong": the KIND of mistake decides what helps. Sahayak now looks at a student's recent mistakes (last 90 days) and, when at least 3 are of one kind and they are at least 40% of the mistakes, names the pattern in plain words and gives a ready plan. Examples of kinds: steps done out of order; slips in arithmetic; careless slips; two ideas mixed up; an earlier skill missing; question misread; knows the idea but struggles to apply it; fact or term not firm; language of the question; answer stops short.
Example plan (steps done out of order): 2 worked examples, then 3 problems done together, then 2 on their own, then a quick check after 2 days and again after 7 days.
WHERE IT SHOWS
1. Tutor / coaching-centre page "Who needs attention": the suggestion names the pattern and adds the plan and a tip. It is also copied into the "Plan an intervention" form.
2. When a parent, tutor or centre opens a linked student: a card "What may help". Parents get a simple at-home tip; tutors and centres get the full plan.
It is never shown to the child as a label, and one or two slips never count as a pattern. It uses fixed rules, so no AI cost.

STEPS
