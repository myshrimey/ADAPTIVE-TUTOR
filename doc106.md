# v106 - Screenshots and pictures in feedback (2026-11-05)

Founder request: in the feedback, users/students/anyone should be able to share screenshots/images and the app should take them in for evaluation and implementation.

## What was built
- Feedback box: "Add a screenshot" (file picker; on a phone it also offers the photo library), paste with Ctrl+V, up to 3 pictures, shrunk in the browser first, with a privacy notice ("cover passwords, phone numbers and other people's names or faces").
- The message is saved first; pictures are sent after, one by one. A failed picture never loses the message and the person is told.
- Server: a short-lived (15 min) signed permission is returned only to the sender of that message. The picture route checks it, checks the first bytes (PNG/JPEG/WebP only; SVG, HTML, GIF, PDF refused), re-encodes as plain JPEG (removes hidden camera/location data), saves to a PRIVATE bucket `feedback-screenshots`. Limits: 3 per message, 10 per internet address per hour, 1.5 MB each.
- Admin: /admin/feedback shows thumbnails ("support" permission), with "Delete picture" (audited). Deleting a family's data also deletes their pictures.
- Migration 0061 `feedback_attachments` (additive, server-only, RLS on, no policy). Until it is applied, text feedback works as before and pictures are politely declined.

## Evidence
- t106: 59 checks (real PNG/JPEG/WebP bytes vs fakes, token sign/expiry/tamper, metadata stripping, fake-storage save/rollback, wiring, migration, bookkeeping).
- Migration run twice (with and without a storage schema), undone and re-applied on a scratch Postgres; RLS on, no policy, bucket private, rows cascade with feedback.
- Full suite: 86 files, 82 passed, 4 known-stale, 0 failed. tsc clean. Production build OK.
- Browser check (Playwright, 375px phone width, pretend server): picker adds thumbnails, remove works, send uploads the picture.

## Not done / not tested
- Not tested on a real phone, with real Supabase storage, or on the live database. Not deployed.
- NO AI reading of screenshots (sending children's images to a third party needs the D7 legal review). "Evaluation" today = support admins look at them.
- No screen-capture button: use the phone/computer screenshot, then Add (or paste). Paste was written but not exercised in the browser check.
- Pictures are not in the student data export; no retention rule yet. Both are D7 items.
